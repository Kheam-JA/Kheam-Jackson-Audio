Shubh Tour

Monitor Engineer

Touring internationally as Monitor Engineer for Shubh, supporting the artist, band and touring production across Australia, New Zealand and Dubai. My role begins well before show day with technical preparation, input planning, console programming and rehearsals, then continues on tour with soundchecks, in-ear monitor mixing, RF coordination, stage integration and real-time technical support throughout every performance.

Australia New Zealand Dubai

Role & Responsibilities

Delivering consistent monitor mixes from rehearsal room to tour stage.

Artist Monitor Mixing

Building and maintaining detailed in-ear mixes for the artist with a focus on vocal clarity, musical balance, confidence and consistency throughout every performance.

Band & Musician Mixes

Managing individual monitor requirements for musicians and performers while maintaining clear communication and fast adjustments during rehearsals, soundchecks and shows.

Tour Preparation

Preparing console files, input lists, output routing, snapshots, patching and technical requirements before travelling so each production day begins from a reliable and repeatable system.

Rehearsals & Programming

Working through rehearsals to refine monitor mixes, establish gain structure, build song-specific workflows and prepare show files before the production reaches the touring stage.

Touring System Integration

Integrating the touring monitor package with local venue infrastructure, stage systems, microphone packages, wireless systems and production audio at each destination.

Show Support & Troubleshooting

Monitoring the entire stage-audio system throughout the performance and responding quickly to artist requests, routing changes, RF issues and technical problems without interrupting the show.
