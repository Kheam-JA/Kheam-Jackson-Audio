CBC

Radio Recording Engineer

Working as a part-time freelance Radio Recording Engineer with CBC, travelling to venues and live production sites across the GTA to capture artists, bands and special performances. My role covers live multitrack recording, microphone deployment, console and signal-flow setup, Pro Tools recording, production coordination and technical troubleshooting, delivering high-quality recordings for mixing and distribution across CBC platforms.

Role & Responsibilities

Capturing live performances for broadcast and digital platforms.

Live Multitrack Recording

Capturing live performances on location with reliable multitrack recording workflows designed for post-production, broadcast and digital distribution.

Avid S6L & Signal Flow

Configuring console routing, stage inputs, recording splits and signal paths to integrate CBC recording systems with venue and production infrastructure.

Pro Tools Recording

Building and managing Pro Tools sessions for live capture, including input organization, track preparation, recording verification and session management.

Microphone Deployment

Selecting and positioning microphones for artists, instruments, audience capture and ambience while adapting to different venues and production requirements.

Production & Venue Integration

Working alongside venue engineers, production companies, artists and CBC teams to integrate recording systems without disrupting the live-show workflow.

Technical Support & Troubleshooting

Managing patching, clocking, digital audio routing, system checks and real-time troubleshooting to maintain reliable recordings throughout production.
