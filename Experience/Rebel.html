Resident Audio Engineer

From Audio Hand to Senior Front of House & Monitor Engineer.

My journey with REBEL Entertainment Complex began in 2016, during the venue's early years following its transformation from the legendary Sound Academy into what is now, one of Canada's premier live music destinations. Starting as an Audio Hand, I worked alongside the installation team helping build the venue's permanent audio infrastructure, including assisting with the installation of the d&b audiotechnik PA system, stage cabling and production wiring.

As the venue evolved, so did my role. Through years of supporting touring productions, festivals and corporate events, I progressed into my current position as Senior Front of House & Monitor Engineer. Today I serve as one of the venue's primary audio engineers, supporting guest engineers, production managers, touring crews, artists and corporate clients throughout every stage of production.

My responsibilities include console preparation, system optimization, stage patching, RF coordination, monitor support, festival changeovers, troubleshooting complex audio systems and ensuring every production integrates seamlessly.

Daily Responsibilities

Supporting every production from load-in to the final encore.

Every production presents different technical requirements. My role is to ensure artists, guest engineers and production teams can focus on delivering great performances while the venue's audio systems operate reliably behind the scenes.

Guest Engineer Support

Assisting visiting Front of House and Monitor Engineers with system integration, show files, patching and venue workflows.

System Preparation

Preparing DiGiCo consoles, verifying signal flow, stage patching, wireless systems and PA readiness before every rehearsal and show.

Live Technical Support

Providing real-time troubleshooting throughout performances while maintaining consistent audio quality under live conditions.

Production Coordination

Working alongside artists, production managers, stage crews, corporate clients and touring personnel from load-in through load-out.

HOUSE PRODUCTION SYSTEMS

Trusted systems for world-class touring productions.

Maintaining and optimizing REBEL's professional audio infrastructure to support international touring artists, festivals, private events and corporate productions every day.

Front of House

Monitor World

PA System

Wireless Systems
